## 1. Root cause (confirmed, unchanged)

See `HW1_INVESTIGATION.md` §1–3. The spurs are images from the two host↔64 kHz conversions:

- linear interpolation on the input side;
- box-integrated ZOH on the output side.

They fold at the incommensurate clock rates. De-emphasis makes them worse and feedback recirculates them. The system is linear and periodically time-varying; the spurs are not caused by nonlinearity.

## 2. Remediation

Architecture choice and alternatives: `HW1_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md`. In summary, both conversions become continuous-time analog-prototype filters, evaluated exactly at the tick and host instants:

- a 7th-order elliptic at 21 kHz with real poles at 50 and 70 kHz;
- latency compensated in whole ticks on the memory path;
- an error-feedforward path at TIME 0;
- the droop EQ refitted to production's tone response, held inside the service-manual band.

Unchanged: the quantiser, gain ranging, emphasis, the AA/reconstruction biquads, LOW/HIGH, feedback, HOLD, modulation, routing and outputs.

No Params were added and none were changed. Static compatibility against the shipped device (`experiments/hw1/results/test_device_compat.json`):

- 162 of 162 parameter definitions identical;
- 4 Push banks identical;
- 120 of 120 factory-program values identical.

## 9. Release recommendation

**REQUEST FURTHER INVESTIGATION.** Do not modify production yet.

The C-engine evidence supports candidate D. It removes HW-1 at every host rate and with modulation, makes the effect path SM-compliant (resolving HW-3), cuts THD+N by a factor of about 50, keeps every timing contract, and preserves the voicing of all nine programs. It cannot be approved for production, for these reasons:

1. **Native validation has not been done.** The brief does not accept C-engine results alone, and no Max 9 / Live 12 run exists. Required: the test device and protocol in `HW1_NATIVE_VALIDATION.md`.
2. **Test-method corrections need an owner decision** for the six measurement-defect tests (#1–#6). Each correction proposed below is at least as strict as the current criterion; none was applied.
   - Measure tones only in `t_filters_static` and `t_wet_gain_and_response`. Production then fails both; HW-2 needs a decision.
   - Use absolute click energy against production in `t_clicks` and `click_excess`.
   - Sweep delay times in the NaN, FB 6.8 and FB 10 scenarios, or replace them with the property actually intended (for example, "inf/NaN ≡ clamp value, bit-exact").
3. **The three bit-identity tests** must be re-baselined against a new reference if D is approved.
4. **Owner listening review** of `experiments/hw1/audio_d/*.flac`, particularly P4, P6 and P8 (top octave) and isolated transients.
5. **CPU:** 3 of 108 cells exceed the 1.5× target. A tabulated exponential would reduce the per-tick cost if Live confirms the overhead.

If items 1–4 pass, approve D as v1.0.3. Promote by porting `make_d.py`'s patch into `tools/make_gen.py`, adding the calibration table to `tools/`, and re-baselining under review. HW-1 stays **open** until then.
